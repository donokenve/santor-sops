Creating a Gmail

How we make the phone's Gmail, in the Gmail app. Every account after it is made from this email. Follow this step by step, every time.

02What this is

One Gmail per phone, made on that phone, in the Gmail app. Instagram, Facebook and Threads are all made from it.

Every login, straight awayThe moment you make an email or a password, it goes in the company login sheet. Before the next step. where the sheet is

03Before you start

  • The phone is set up (SOP: Setting Up the Phone) and the Gmail app is up to date.
  • The phone is on a hotspot.
  • Your model: her name (her model deck).
  • The company login sheet open.

05Done means

  • One Gmail, made on this phone in the Gmail app.
  • No phone number on it.
  • Email and password in the company login sheet.
  • Next: Creating an Instagram.

06Common mistakes

  • Adding a phone number. Skip it.
  • Writing the password down "later". The sheet, straight away.
