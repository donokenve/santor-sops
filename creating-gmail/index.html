Creating a Gmail

How we make the phone's Gmail, in the Gmail app. Every account after it is made from this email. Watch the video first, then follow this step by step, every time.

Version v0.1Updated 10 Nov 2026Owner KFor postersTool Gmail app · Samsung

01Watch first

Creating a Gmail. Watch it once for the flow.

⚠ Read this before you watchThe video uses an iPhone. We use the Gmail app on the Samsung. Watch it for the flow, then follow the steps below. If they're different, the steps win.

Video not playing? Open it here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g3oJ2H9KdyM

02What this is

One Gmail per phone, made on that phone, in the Gmail app. It is her email: her name, a girly address. Instagram, Facebook and Threads are all made from it.

⚠ The rules that get accounts banned

NEVER CONNECT THE PHONE TO WI-FI. HOTSPOT ONLY.

NEVER DOWNLOAD A PICTURE FROM GOOGLE DRIVE ONTO THE PHONE. ALWAYS OPEN IT AND TAKE A SCREENSHOT.

The apps can see where a picture came from and how the phone connects. Break one of these and the account gets banned.
Every login, straight awayThe moment you make an email or a password, it goes in the company login sheet. Before the next step. where the sheet is

03Before you start

  • The phone is set up (SOP: Setting Up the Phone) and the Gmail app is up to date.
  • The phone is on a hotspot.
  • You know which model this phone is for: her name and her niche (her model deck).
  • The company login sheet open.

04Step by step

1

Open the Gmail app

On the home screen, open the Google folder and press Gmail.

The screens may look a little different on your phone. It's the same thing: follow the words.

2

Add an email address

Gmail is empty. Press Add an email address.

3

Choose Google

Set up email lists Google, Outlook, Yahoo, Exchange and Other. Press Google.

4

Create account, for myself

Press Create account → For myself. From here, the screens are the same as in the video.

5

Her name

Always the name of the model this phone belongs to. First name and last name, as in her model deck.

6

Birthday: she is 19 or 20

Her age is always 19 or 20. In 2026, any birthday in 2006 works: she is 19 or 20 all year.

Examples: 14 March 2006 · 2 June 2006 · 23 September 2006 · 8 December 2006. Gender: female.

From 1 January 2027 it becomes 2007. Rule: born 19–20 years before today. (The video puts 1950. Not us: 2006.)

7

A girly email

Her name, a touch of her niche, and numbers. Girly, easy to read. If Google says it's taken, add or change the numbers.

Examples: ellathrones416@gmail.com · chloeelwood444@gmail.com · sophierosewoodlotr@gmail.com · gracetaylorsw@gmail.com

8

A strong, simple password

Strong but simple: one you can write down correctly and remember. Into the company login sheet now, letter for letter.

9

Never a phone number

After the password, it may ask to verify a phone number. Never add one. Press the blue Skip at the bottom left. Skip everything else too: recovery email, extras.

No Skip button? Stop. Ask K or Leon to help.

10

Never back up

Google Services: Back up to Google Drive: turn it off. Then press Accept.

11

Take me to Gmail

Her email is now on the list. Press Take me to Gmail. Done.

05Done means

  • One Gmail, made on this phone in the Gmail app.
  • Her name, a girly email, age 19 or 20.
  • No phone number on it. Back up to Google Drive off.
  • Email and password in the company login sheet, exactly right.
  • Next: Creating an Instagram.

06Common mistakes

  • A complex password you can't write down or remember.
  • The password noted wrong in the sheet. Check it letter for letter.
  • Adding a phone number. Never.
  • Leaving Back up to Google Drive on. Turn it off.
  • A name that isn't the phone's model.
  • A birthday that makes her under 19 or over 20.
