02Profile and page

Facebook is two things: her profile and her page. They are different.

The profile · the land

Every page needs a profile to own it. Fans never see it. It stays plain: her name, no profile picture needed, a few niche pages followed. It just has to exist and look like a person.

The page · the house

What fans see and follow. Built properly from day one: profile picture, a niche banner, a bio, category, posts. This is where the work goes.

The profile is the land. The page is the house built on it.

⚠ The rules that get accounts banned

NEVER CONNECT THE PHONE TO WI-FI. HOTSPOT ONLY.

NEVER DOWNLOAD A PICTURE FROM GOOGLE DRIVE ONTO THE PHONE. ALWAYS OPEN IT AND TAKE A SCREENSHOT.

The apps can see where a picture came from and how the phone connects. Break one of these and the account gets banned.

03Before you start

  • Her Instagram is made (SOP: Creating an Instagram).
  • The phone is on a hotspot.
  • Her Gmail, password and birthday from the company login sheet. Facebook uses the same.
  • For the page: her profile picture and a niche banner, ready in Google Drive (SOP: Creating Pictures).

04Step by step: the profile

1

Join Facebook

Open Facebook. Press Create new account, then Get started.

2

Her full name

First name and surname: her full name, as in her model deck.

3

Birthday: the same as the Gmail

The same date as her Gmail (she is 19 or 20).

4

Gender: female

Press Female.

5

Sign up with email

It asks for a mobile number. Never. Press Sign up with email address.

Why: a number ties the account to a real SIM and to every other account on it.

6

Her Gmail

The same Gmail as her Instagram.

7

Password: the same as the Gmail

The same password as her Gmail and Instagram.

8

Save login info

Press Save.

9

Agree

Press I agree.

10

No, create new account

"Do you already have a Facebook account?" Press No, create new account.

11

The code

Open the Gmail app, copy the code Facebook sent, put it in.

12

Profile picture: skip

The profile doesn't need one. Press Skip.

13

Contacts: never

Turn on contact uploading? Never. Press Not now or Skip.

Why: contacts link the account to real people and to the other phones.

14

Meta account: always No

Facebook sees her Instagram uses the same email and asks to connect them in a Meta account. Always press No.

Why: connected accounts fall together. If one gets banned, they all get banned.

Screenshot · fb-14 · connect Meta account: No
15

Notifications: on

Allow notifications.

16

10 minutes of warm up

Follow a few pages related to her niche. Scroll a little, like a person would. About 10 minutes. That's all the profile needs.

06Done means

  • A profile and a page, made with her Gmail, same password, same birthday.
  • No phone number. No contacts. No Meta account connection.
  • The profile: her name, 10 minutes of warm up, niche pages followed.
  • The page: profile picture and niche banner (screenshots, never downloads), bio, Digital creator + Personal blog.
  • Notifications on. Page name in the company login sheet.

07Common mistakes

  • Pressing Yes to the Meta account. One ban, all banned.
  • A phone number instead of the email.
  • No banner on the page. An empty cover looks fake.
  • Downloading the picture from Drive instead of a screenshot.
