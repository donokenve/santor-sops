Creating Pictures

How we make every picture for an account in the Factory: profile pictures, image posts and link stories. Watch the video first. Then follow this step by step, every time.

02What this is

Pictures do three things for an account: profile pictures, image posts and link stories. The profile picture establishes who she is. The posts give the profile life and give the model a personality. The stories bring the fan in: they click the story, then the link.

On Threads, pictures matter the most. They're also used in replies, and replies are a big part of what gets an account attention.

03Before you start

  • You're logged in to the Factory and you're on the Pictures tab.
  • Pinterest is open in another tab.
  • You know your model: who she is, her niche, her personality and where she'd take pictures (the model deck).
  • You have a clear picture of her face ready to use as the reference.
  • For an outfit change: you have the outfit reference picture ready.
  • You have the two prompts (section 07). They're on your task board too.

05The 3 rules of a picture

Every original you pick, and every picture you keep, passes all three:

She's doing somethingNot posing for a photo shoot. Having a coffee, going to bed, dressed up at a party, a selfie in her room.
She's relatively sexySexy, but still innocent and playful. Girly and cute.
It makes sense to the nicheAnd to her. A Star Wars girl isn't swimming in a pool. Would she really be there?
Above allIt must feel real. Natural, normal, the kind of photo a real girl posts.

06Step by step

1

Find the original on Pinterest

Search with simple words: daily life, coffee date, morning, going to bed, or the niche plus cosplay. When you see one you like, click it: Pinterest then shows you more like it, and over time your feed tailors itself to each model. Download it.

Plus points if the girl in the original is already doing what you want your model doing.

Green: good originals. She is doing something, it looks real, it isn't a photo shoot. Red: skip them.

2

Check it against the 3 rules

Doing something, relatively sexy, makes sense to the niche and to her. If it looks like a photo shoot, skip it. Each model has her places: one is at home and outside, one is innocent and at work, one is in nature.

Every model has her own model deck: who she is, her niche, the places she's in and her reference accounts. Check the original fits her. Don't know her places? Ask K or Leon before you generate.

3

Decide: face only, or face and outfit

Face only: you like the outfit, the look and the shadows. Only her face changes. Easier.
Face and outfit: you want her in one of our outfits. Her face and the outfit both go onto the photo.

4

Upload in the Factory

Pictures tab. Upload the original. Add her clear face picture as a reference, and the outfit picture if you're changing the outfit.

Check the order before you generate. Girl one is your model. Girl two is the original. The prompt depends on it.

Screenshot · pictures-step-4 · upload and reference order
5

Paste the prompt

Face only → prompt B. Face and outfit → prompt A. Add anything extra at the end (section 07).

Screenshot · pictures-step-5 · prompt box
6

Generate: 6 at most

Run a batch so you get options. Six is the maximum for one original. While it runs, go back to step 1 for the next one.

7

Review every result

The AI messes up very often. That's normal: just run it again. Keep only the ones that feel real (section 08). Delete the rest.

8

Save the keepers

Save every keeper. If two or three go together, save them as a set for one carousel post. where they're saved: Drive folder name

07Prompts

Copy them exactly. They're also on your task board. Missing? Ask Claudya or K.

A · Face and outfit

Put the subject identity of girl one on the subject identity of person two in this image. Maintain girl one outfit. Maintain photo two quality and setting. Keep it natural.

B · Face only

Put face of girl one onto face of girl two. Maintain photo two quality and outfit. Keep it natural.

K: check both against the task-board versions (written from the video)

Building on top

Anything you want different, add it to the end of the prompt: a blue lightsaber, red hair, white shoes, someone in the back smiling at the camera. The two prompts are the baseline. Everything else builds on them.

08Good vs bad

The test: if you saw this on her profile, would you believe she's real? At the start, K tells you why each one is good or bad. Soon you judge it yourself.

Keep
  • It feels real, like a real girl's photo.
  • It gives her personality: playful, smiling, girly.
  • It fits the niche and the place makes sense.
  • The details match: colours, light, the friend next to her.
Example · pictures-good-1
Delete
  • It looks fake. You feel it straight away.
  • Her face quality doesn't match the background.
  • Her face doesn't match the light.
  • Her face shape doesn't match the body.
  • Her size is wrong for the room: too big, too small.

09Done means

  • The number of pictures your task asks for.
  • A good mix: girly, sexy, cute, speaking the niche, and they all look real.
  • Every one passes the 3 rules.
  • Saved where they belong. folder
  • Reviewed with K.

10Common mistakes

  • The reference order is wrong. Girl one must be your model.
  • More than 6 at once. Six is the maximum.
  • A place that makes no sense for her niche or for her.
  • A photo-shoot look. We want normal, everyday photos.
  • Keeping a "nearly real" one. If it feels off, delete it.
