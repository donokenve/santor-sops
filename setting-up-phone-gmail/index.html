Setting Up the Phone + Gmail

How we set up a new Samsung and make its Gmail. Every account starts here. Watch the video first, then follow this step by step, every time.

02What this is

Every account lives on its own phone. The phone has to look like a normal person's phone: no Samsung account, nothing shared, nothing linked. Then we make one Gmail. Instagram, Facebook and Threads are all made from that Gmail.

Above allThe phone never touches Wi-Fi. Not once, not for a second. Hotspot only: your own phone's, or another work phone's.

03Before you start

  • The Samsung, charged, with its SIM card in.
  • A hotspot you can use: your own phone, or another work phone.
  • The company login sheet open on your laptop.
  • Your model: her name and her niche (her model deck).

04Step by step

1

Turn it on: English, United States

Language and region: English, United States. Press Start.

Screenshot · phone-1 · language and region
2

Licence and privacy

Do not tick diagnostic data. Leave it empty. Press Accept.

Screenshot · phone-2 · licence screen, diagnostic data off
3

Set up manually

Don't copy anything from another phone. Choose to set it up yourself.

Screenshot · phone-3 · set up manually
4

Wi-Fi: skip

It will ask you to connect to Wi-Fi. Skip. Never connect to Wi-Fi, at any point.

Screenshot · phone-4 · Wi-Fi screen, skip
5

Skip, skip

Skip the next screens. Don't sign in to anything yet.

Screenshot · phone-5 · screens to skip
6

Turn everything off

On the services screen, turn every toggle off: location, scanning, backup, sending data.

Screenshot · phone-6 · every toggle off
7

No Samsung account

Don't open a Samsung account. Don't give any information. Skip everything until you're on the home screen.

Screenshot · phone-7 · Samsung account, skip
8

Connect to the hotspot

Now, on the home screen, connect to the hotspot. Only the hotspot.

Screenshot · phone-8 · connected to hotspot
9

Make the Gmail

Open the Play Store and sign in with Google → Create account → For myself. Her name, then type the email you want. Save. Make a password. Save. Skip everything else (phone number, extras).

Email: the format for the email name

Screenshot · phone-9 · create the Gmail
10

Put it in the login sheet

Email and password go in the company login sheet now, with the phone it's on.

11

Download the apps

In the Play Store: Gmail, Instagram, Facebook, Threads. Nothing else.

Screenshot · phone-10 · the 4 apps on the home screen

05Done means

  • The phone is on the home screen and has never been on Wi-Fi.
  • No Samsung account.
  • One Gmail, made on this phone.
  • Gmail, Instagram, Facebook and Threads installed.
  • The email and password are in the company login sheet.

06Common mistakes

  • Connecting to the office Wi-Fi "just for a second". Never.
  • Ticking diagnostic data or leaving a toggle on.
  • Making a Samsung account. Skip it.
  • Writing the password down "later". It goes in the sheet straight away.
