Posting on Threads

How she makes a Threads post and how she makes a reply under other people’s posts: what to write, when, where, and exactly how. Watch first, then follow this step by step, every time.

02What this is

Threads is about conversations. Her Threads posts bring people to her profile. Her replies under other people’s posts bring even more: replies are about half of everything people see on Threads. And on Threads, pictures matter the most, in her posts and in her replies.

Threads is sexier than Instagram Wow pictures: her sexiest pictures, the ones that make someone stop scrolling.
Flirty, very clear captions. Her Threads posts can be random; her niche shows in who she is and where she replies, so the right people find her.
Replies win it: a reply that fits the post, with a sexy picture of her.

Threads brings fans two ways: a post or a reply can go viral and bring many new fans, and fans from her Instagram tap through to her Threads and see more of her there. So her Threads always has to be full and worth the tap.

There are two things you do here, and they are different:

  • A Threads post: on her own profile. A clear, flirty line (it can be random), with a wow picture.
  • A reply: under someone else’s post that is getting attention. Her answer to that exact post, with a sexy picture of her.
The Threads rules Starts on day 4, after her first Instagram reel. Before that: warm up only (SOP: Warming Up Threads).
How many a day: 3 Threads posts + 15 replies. From 200 followers: 5 Threads posts + 20 replies. Your task says.
At least 20 minutes between two Threads posts, with replies and warm up in between. Never back to back.
Replies spread through her session, never 15 in a row. K: how fast is too fast for replies?
Never before 8:00pm Bali. K: same 8pm rule as Instagram for Threads?
⚠ The rules that get accounts banned

NEVER CONNECT THE PHONE TO WI-FI. HOTSPOT ONLY.

NEVER DOWNLOAD A PICTURE FROM GOOGLE DRIVE ONTO THE PHONE. ALWAYS OPEN IT AND TAKE A SCREENSHOT.

The apps can see where a picture came from and how the phone connects. Break one of these and the account gets banned.

03How Threads picks who sees her

  • Strangers see her in the For You feed. Threads guesses if they will like it, reply, open her profile, or scroll past. Write for a stranger, not for her followers.
  • The first minutes count. Right after her post goes up, the replies she answers straight away push it further. So she stays and answers.
  • One topic, not hashtags. Every Threads post gets one topic: her niche.
  • Begging buries her. "Like if you…", "follow for more", "comment yes": Threads shows those to fewer people.
  • Replying early wins. A reply in the first hour of a post that is taking off is seen by everyone who reads that post. A reply on a dead post is seen by nobody.

04Before you start

  • It’s day 4 or later, and her first Instagram reel is up.
  • It’s 8:00pm Bali or later.
  • The phone is on a hotspot.
  • You’re in her Threads on her phone (check the name on her profile).
  • Her pictures are in her Google Drive folder (SOPs: Creating Pictures, Google Drive).
  • Today’s warm up is done (SOP: Warming Up Threads).

05Step by step: a Threads post

1

Pick the picture, write the line

Pick a wow picture from her Drive folder: one of her sexiest. Then write the caption with ChatGPT, always: flirty and very clear, one or two short lines, the way she talks.

The line can be random: a thought, a mood, something from her day, a flirty question. It doesn’t have to be about her niche. The wow picture and the flirty tone do the work.

the ChatGPT prompt for Threads captions, and examples that worked

Why: strangers decide in a second. A wow picture and a clear, flirty line make them stop and open her profile.

2

Screenshot it from Drive

Open the picture in Google Drive, full screen. Take a screenshot. Crop it so only the picture is left.

NEVER download it from Google Drive. Always the screenshot.

3

Open Threads → new thread

In Threads, press the + (new thread) at the bottom.

Screenshot · tp-1 · the + for a new thread
4

Write the line, add the picture

Type her line. Press the picture icon under it and pick your screenshot (the first one in Recents).

Screenshot · tp-2 · line written, picture added
5

Add a topic: her niche

Press Add a topic and pick one that is her niche (e.g. Star Wars, cosplay).

No hashtags. One topic per Threads post.

Screenshot · tp-3 · Add a topic
6

Press Post

You are making a Threads post. Press Post. Check it is on her profile, looking right.

Screenshot · tp-4 · the Post button
7

Stay: answer the replies

Stay on Threads. Every reply on her post gets an answer from her, in the first 30 minutes: real, short, like a girl talking to someone. K: how long she stays

Why: Threads pushes posts where the girl answers straight away. Posting and leaving kills the post.

8

The next Threads post: 20 minutes or more

At least 20 minutes later, with replies and warm up in between. Then start again from step 1.

06Step by step: a reply

1

Find a post that is taking off

In For You or by searching her niche, find a post that is new (last hour or two) and getting a lot of replies: a big account in her niche, or a funny, cute or feel-good post.

K: the reference accounts and where to find good posts

Why: an early reply on a busy post sits near the top, where everyone reading it sees her.

2

Check it is safe

Look at the post and the replies. If it is on the never list below, scroll on.

3

Press the reply bubble

Under the post, press the speech bubble.

Screenshot · tr-1 · the reply bubble
4

Write her reply

It has to fit that post: answer what it says, flirty, playful, her voice. Add a sexy picture of her, one that fits what the post is about. K: what a good reply is, with examples; ChatGPT for replies too?

  • Never "so true", "😍", "nice", never the same reply twice.
  • Never her link, never "check my profile".
  • Never her age.
Screenshot · tr-2 · her reply, with a picture
5

Press Post

You are making a reply. Press Post. Then move on: like a few things, scroll, find the next post.

Screenshot · tr-3 · the reply posted
6

Spread them out

Her replies for the day go in between her Threads posts and her warm up, never all in one go.

Why: 15 replies in 5 minutes is what a bot does.

07Where she never replies

⚠ Never reply under these

CHILDREN · VIOLENCE (TO PEOPLE OR ANIMALS) · DISASTERS OR WAR · POLITICS OR ATTACKS ON PUBLIC FIGURES

A reply there makes her account look wrong and gets it reported.

Good places: her niche, funny posts, animals, people doing something nice.

Never write her age in a reply. Not even as a joke.

09Done means

  • Today’s Threads posts up (3, or 5 from 200 followers), each with a picture and one topic.
  • At least 20 minutes between two Threads posts.
  • Every reply on her posts answered.
  • Today’s replies done (15, or 20 from 200 followers), spread out, none on the never list.
  • Pictures from screenshots, never downloads.
  • A screenshot of her posts in your task. how replies are shown in the task

10Common mistakes

  • Posting before day 4.
  • Two Threads posts back to back.
  • 15 replies in a row.
  • Copy-paste replies ("so true", emojis only).
  • Replying on old, dead posts.
  • Hashtags instead of one topic.
  • Begging for likes or follows.
  • Posting and leaving without answering her replies.
