02What this is

Threads runs on her Instagram: you log in with it. On Threads, pictures matter the most, in posts and in replies. Make the Instagram first.

⚠ The rules that get accounts banned

NEVER CONNECT THE PHONE TO WI-FI. HOTSPOT ONLY.

NEVER DOWNLOAD A PICTURE FROM GOOGLE DRIVE ONTO THE PHONE. ALWAYS OPEN IT AND TAKE A SCREENSHOT.

The apps can see where a picture came from and how the phone connects. Break one of these and the account gets banned.

03Before you start

  • Her Instagram is made, on this phone (SOP: Creating an Instagram).
  • The phone is on the hotspot.
  • Her pictures are ready (SOP: Creating Pictures).

5

Hide AI comments

Do this the moment the account exists. Before anything gets posted.

Fans who find her have to feel she is real. One "this is AI" comment under a post and everyone who reads it starts to wonder. So the account hides those comments before anyone sees them.

Where: Profile → ☰ → Privacy → Hidden Words → Manage custom words and phrases.

Paste this list in, then turn Hide comments ON:

ai, a.i, a.i., fake, deepfake, bot, scam, gpt, chatgpt, slop, faked, not real, midjourney

Into the login sheet
