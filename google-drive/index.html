02What this is

Every picture and video you make goes into Google Drive, in the right folder. The more you make, the more this matters: a messy Drive means lost pictures and the wrong picture on the wrong account.

Drive is also how a picture gets from the laptop to the phone you post from.

⚠ The rules that get accounts banned

NEVER CONNECT THE PHONE TO WI-FI. HOTSPOT ONLY.

NEVER DOWNLOAD A PICTURE FROM GOOGLE DRIVE ONTO THE PHONE. ALWAYS OPEN IT AND TAKE A SCREENSHOT.

The apps can see where a picture came from and how the phone connects. Break one of these and the account gets banned.

03Before you start

  • Your laptop, signed in to the Drive account: which Google account
  • The Google Drive app on the posting phone (Play Store).
  • Which model and which accounts your work is for.

05Step by step

1

Open Drive

On the laptop: drive.google.com, or the 9-dot menu at the top right of any Google page → Drive.

2

Go to the right folder

My Drive → her model's folder → the right kind (pictures, videos…). Never upload into My Drive itself.

3

A new folder, only if it's missing

New → Folder. Name it exactly like the others. folder naming rule

4

Upload

Drag the files from your laptop straight into the folder. Or New → File upload.

5

Share it

Right-click the folder → Share. Add who needs it: who gets access, and as what Never "Anyone with the link".

Why: anyone with the link means anyone, forever.

6

On the phone: open, never download

On the posting phone, open the Google Drive app → the folder → tap the picture so it's full screen.

Take a screenshot (power + volume down). Crop it in the Gallery so only the picture is left. Use that.

NEVER DOWNLOAD A PICTURE FROM GOOGLE DRIVE. ALWAYS THE SCREENSHOT.

Why: a downloaded file keeps where it came from. A screenshot is a fresh picture taken on this phone.

06Done means

  • Everything you made today is in Drive, in her folder, in the right kind.
  • Named like the rest. Nothing loose in My Drive.
  • Shared with the right people only.
  • Pictures reached the phone by screenshot, never download.

07Common mistakes

  • Downloading from Drive onto the phone. Always the screenshot.
  • Files loose in My Drive instead of her folder.
  • One model's pictures in another model's folder.
  • Sharing with "Anyone with the link".
