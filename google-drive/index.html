02What this is

Every picture and video you make goes into Drive, in the folder of the account it is for. Then the phone that posts for that account opens the same folder.

Why: the more you make, the more this matters. One folder per phone means the right picture always reaches the right account, nothing gets posted twice, and anyone can pick up where you left off.

⚠ The rules that get accounts banned

NEVER CONNECT THE PHONE TO WI-FI. HOTSPOT ONLY.

NEVER DOWNLOAD A PICTURE FROM GOOGLE DRIVE ONTO THE PHONE. ALWAYS OPEN IT AND TAKE A SCREENSHOT.

The apps can see where a picture came from and how the phone connects. Break one of these and the account gets banned.

03The folders

Every model is set up the same way. Copy this exactly: names in [brackets] are replaced with the real name.

My Drive
📁 [MODEL NAME] Kiraone per model
📁 [MAIN SOCIAL HANDLE] kiraofvulcannone per phone · this is the folder you share
📁 [FB HANDLE] [FB] Kira Of Vulcan [FB]
📁 Posts/Carousels
📁 Reels → 📁 Posted
📁 [IG/T HANDLE] [IG/T] kiraofvulcann [IG/T]Instagram + Threads
📁 Posts/Carousels
📁 Reels → 📁 Trial Reels · 📁 Posted
📁 Link Stories
📁 Profile Pictures & Banners

Why one [MAIN SOCIAL HANDLE] folder per phone: each phone runs one set of accounts (Facebook + Instagram/Threads). Sharing just that folder with that phone means the phone only ever sees its own content.

Why "Posted": when something goes up, move it into Posted. What’s left outside is what’s still to post. Nothing gets posted twice.

04Step by step: set it up (once per phone)

1

Open Drive on the laptop

drive.google.com, signed in to the company account.

2

The model folder

In My Drive: New → Folder, named with her name: Kira. Only if it doesn’t exist yet: one per model.

3

The phone folder

Inside her folder: New → Folder, named with the main handle of this phone’s accounts: kiraofvulcann.

Why: a model can have several phones. Each phone gets its own folder so their content never mixes.

4

The four folders inside

Inside the phone folder, make these four:

  • [FB HANDLE] [FB] e.g. Kira Of Vulcan [FB]
  • [IG/T HANDLE] [IG/T] e.g. kiraofvulcann [IG/T]
  • Link Stories
  • Profile Pictures & Banners

Why: Facebook and Instagram/Threads get different content. Stories and profile pictures are used by all of them, so they sit on their own.

5

Inside [FB] and [IG/T]

In each: Posts/Carousels and Reels. Inside Reels: Posted. On [IG/T] also Trial Reels.

6

Share the phone folder with the phone

Right-click the phone folder (kiraofvulcann) → Share. Type the Gmail you created on that phone. Keep General access Restricted. Done.

Why: the phone sees this folder under Shared, and only this folder. Restricted means nobody else can open it with a link.

05Step by step: every day

1

Upload into the right folder

On the laptop: drag what you made into the right folder: a reel into [IG/T] → Reels, a carousel into Posts/Carousels, a story picture into Link Stories.

2

On the phone: Drive → Shared

Open the Google Drive app on the posting phone → Shared → the phone folder.

It asks to unlock storage with a phone number: I’ll do it later. Never a phone number.

3

Open, screenshot, post

Open the picture full screen. Take a screenshot (power + volume down), crop it in the Gallery, and post that.

NEVER DOWNLOAD A PICTURE FROM GOOGLE DRIVE. ALWAYS THE SCREENSHOT.

Why: a downloaded file keeps where it came from. A screenshot is a fresh picture taken on this phone.

videos: how they get onto the phone
4

Move it to Posted

Once it’s up, move it into Posted.

Why: what’s outside Posted is still to post. Nothing gets posted twice.

06Done means

  • Her folder, the phone folder, and the four folders inside, named exactly like the tree.
  • The phone folder is shared with that phone’s Gmail only, Restricted.
  • Everything you made is in its folder. Nothing loose in My Drive.
  • Posted things are in Posted. Pictures reached the phone by screenshot.

07Common mistakes

  • Downloading from Drive onto the phone. Always the screenshot.
  • Sharing the whole model folder instead of the phone folder: the phone sees other phones' content.
  • Forgetting to move to Posted. It gets posted twice.
  • "Anyone with the link". Always Restricted.
  • Giving Drive a phone number. I’ll do it later.
